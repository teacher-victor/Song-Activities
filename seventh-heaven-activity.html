A restless song about grinding through a tired, underpaid life and wanting one wild, reckless night to make up for it.

Genre: Alt-pop / hip-hop Theme: Burnout & chasing one wild night Level: B1–B2

What you'll do

Read through the lyrics of "Seventh Heaven" and fill in the missing words using the board on the side. Every blank has one correct answer taken straight from the song. When you're done, check your answers and see how the ride went, plus a quick reference to every word you just practiced.

How to play

  1. Click a word on the board to select it. It will light up to show it's chosen.
  2. Click a blank in the lyrics to drop that word into it. Click a filled blank again to pull the word back out.
  3. Changed your mind about a tile before placing it? Click it again to deselect.
  4. Once you're happy with your answers, click Check Answers.

The board has more words than blanks. Some are there to test you, not to be used.
