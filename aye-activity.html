What you'll do

Read through the lyrics of "Aye" and fill in the missing words using the board on the side. Every blank has one correct answer taken straight from the song. This track deals with heavy, real-world subject matter and uses strong language, so read it with that in mind. When you're done, check your answers and see how well you tracked the language, plus a quick reference to every word you just practiced.

How to play

  1. Click a word on the board to select it. It will light up to show it's chosen.
  2. Click a blank in the lyrics to drop that word into it. Click a filled blank again to pull the word back out.
  3. Changed your mind about a tile before placing it? Click it again to deselect.
  4. Once you're happy with your answers, click Check Answers.

The board has more words than blanks. Some are there to test you, not to be used.

About the song

"Aye" is by Sam Fender. It's a dense, furious piece of social commentary that moves through historical violence, media spin, and class resentment before spiraling into a kind of exhausted self-negation by the end. The lyrics reference real historical events and figures directly, so some lines are heavy by design.
