What you'll do

Read through the lyrics of "Futureproof" and fill in the missing words using the board on the side. Every blank has one correct answer taken straight from the song. When you're done, check your answers and see how well you know the track, plus a quick reference to every word you just practiced.

How to play

  1. Click a word on the board to select it. It will light up to show it's chosen.
  2. Click a blank in the lyrics to drop that word into it. Click a filled blank again to pull the word back out.
  3. Changed your mind about a tile before placing it? Click it again to deselect.
  4. Once every blank feels right (or you want to see how you're doing), click Check Answers.

The board has more words than blanks. Some are there to test you, not to be used.

About the song

"Futureproof" is by The Rare Occasions, an American indie rock band. The song sits with someone stuck replaying an old relationship, comparing the permanence of a saved photo to the way youth and love actually slip away. It builds around the image of an open road and a promise to keep moving forward, even while looking back.
