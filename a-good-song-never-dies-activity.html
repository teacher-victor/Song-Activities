A song that pushes back on the music industry's cash-and-hype machine, insisting that what actually lasts is the moment a song first made you feel something. Fill in the lyrics using the word board on the right.

Genre: Indie Pop / Rock Theme: Authenticity vs. hype Level: B1-B2

Fill in the Blanks

Listen to the song with your teacher, then complete the lyrics using the words on the board. The blanks are spread across the whole song, so keep an eye out all the way to the end. The board stays on the right so you can check it while you scroll.

How to play

1

Read the short definition on each tile in the word board first, so you know what a word means before you use it.

2

Click a word tile to select it. It lights up in gold to show it's ready to go.

3

Click a blank in the lyrics to drop that word in. A few words come up more than once, so their tile stays active until every one of its blanks is filled.

4

Changed your mind? Click a filled blank again and the word hops right back to the board.

5

Click "Check Answers" whenever you're ready, even with a few blanks still empty. Those just count as incorrect, and you'll see the right word appear beside them.

Tip The board holds more words than the song has blanks. A handful are distractors that don't belong anywhere in the lyrics, so trust the definitions, not just the vibe.
