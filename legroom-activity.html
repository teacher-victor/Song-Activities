What you'll do

Read through the lyrics of "legroom" and fill in the missing words using the board on the side. Every blank has one correct answer taken straight from the song. When you're done, check your answers and see how well you know the track, plus a quick reference to every word you just practiced.

How to play

  1. Click a word on the board to select it. It will light up to show it's chosen.
  2. Click a blank in the lyrics to drop that word into it. Click a filled blank again to pull the word back out.
  3. Changed your mind about a tile before placing it? Click it again to deselect.
  4. Once you're happy with your answers, click Check Answers.

The board has more words than blanks. Some are there to test you, not to be used.

About the song

"legroom" is by Adan Diaz. It's a quiet, intimate song about the specific, physical ways someone's absence shows up afterward: an empty side of the bed, an untouched car seat, more space than you know what to do with.
