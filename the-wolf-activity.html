What you'll do

Read through the lyrics of "The Wolf" and fill in the missing words using the board on the side. Every blank has one correct answer taken straight from the song. When you're done, check your answers and see how well you tracked it down, plus a quick reference to every word you practiced.

How to play

  1. Click a word on the board to select it. It will light up to show it's chosen.
  2. Click a blank in the lyrics to drop that word into it. Click a filled blank again to pull the word back out.
  3. Changed your mind about a tile before placing it? Click it again to deselect.
  4. Once you're happy with your answers, click Check Answers.

The board has more words than blanks. Some are there to test you, not to be used.

About the song

"The Wolf" is by Siamés, a duo known for blending electronic production with moody, atmospheric pop songwriting. The lyrics lean into obsession and pursuit, framing wanting someone as something instinctive and a little dangerous, like a hunt that never quite ends.
